HOW VRP WORKS

Traditional networking binds the logical session directly to the transport. When the transport fails, applications often reconnect, rebuild state or restart execution. VRP introduces a different model. The transport becomes replaceable. The session remains canonical.

Traditional Model

CLIENT
↓
TRANSPORT
↓
TRANSPORT FAILURE
↓
SESSION INTERRUPTED

VRP Continuity Model

CLIENT
↓
CANONICAL SESSION
↓
TRANSPORT A
↓
TRANSPORT B
↓
SESSION PRESERVED
WITHOUT CONTINUITY-FIRST
  • Transport becomes the execution boundary
  • Reconnect may rebuild application state
  • Recovery depends on application behaviour
  • Session continuity is not guaranteed
WITH VRP
  • Session identity remains canonical
  • Transport is replaceable
  • Replay attempts are rejected
  • Recovery focuses on continuity instead of restart

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

The following principles define the public engineering philosophy of VRP. They describe architectural intent rather than protected implementation.

01
SESSION ≠ TRANSPORT

Logical session identity is treated independently from the underlying transport.

02
CONTINUITY FIRST

Temporary transport disruption should not define logical execution.

03
EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS

Engineering statements should be supported by reproducible validation.

04
DETERMINISTIC BEHAVIOUR

Predictable execution is preferred over implementation complexity.

05
PUBLIC ARCHITECTURE

Architecture may be reviewed independently without requiring access to the protected runtime.

06
PROTECTED IMPLEMENTATION

Observable behaviour is public. Protected implementation remains private.
