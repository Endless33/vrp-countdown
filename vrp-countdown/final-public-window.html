01 / ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATION

The session survives beyond the transport.

In transport-coupled systems, network interruption may force session reconstruction, application reconnection or repeated execution.

VRP separates logical session identity from the underlying transport and defines controlled transitions for migration, recovery and authority. Preservation depends on the relevant runtime conditions and successful validation.
