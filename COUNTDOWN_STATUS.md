# Enterprise Pilot Window Update

## Current Enterprise Pilot Window

The current enterprise pilot window for organizations with **10,001+ employees** is scheduled to remain open until:

**September 30, 2026 — 00:00 UTC**

This date is subject to change.

The enterprise pilot window may close earlier or remain open longer depending on engineering priorities, pilot capacity, project direction, and ongoing evaluations.

No additional notice is guaranteed.

---

## Enterprise Evaluation

Organizations with **10,001+ employees** are encouraged to begin evaluation before the current enterprise pilot window ends.

After the enterprise pilot window closes, new enterprise pilot intake is expected to be **suspended**.

The suspension may remain in effect for approximately **6–12 months**, while work continues with existing pilot partners and protocol development.

---

## During Suspension

- Existing enterprise pilot evaluations continue.
- Active pilot partners continue to receive support.
- Protocol development continues.
- Runtime validation continues.
- Public evidence and documentation remain available.
- Protected Core remains protected.

---

## Engineering Priority

Engineering time is intentionally focused on:

- Existing pilot partners
- Runtime development
- Protected Core hardening
- Runtime validation
- Protocol evolution
- Production readiness

The objective is to complete existing evaluations before accepting additional enterprise pilot participants.

---

## Public / Private Boundary

Public Evidence remains public.

Protected Core remains protected.

The availability of public evidence does not imply access to Protected Core implementation, private runtime components, or internal engineering materials.

---

## Enterprise Intake Policy

The enterprise pilot window is intentionally flexible.

It may close earlier than September 30, remain open longer, or transition directly into suspension without extending the current intake period.

No organization is guaranteed evaluation simply because the enterprise pilot window remains open.

Pilot capacity remains intentionally limited.

---

## Suspension Policy

Following suspension of enterprise pilot intake:

- New enterprise applications may not be reviewed.
- New enterprise pilot participation may not be available.
- Future enterprise intake dates are not guaranteed.
- Future pricing, participation rules, evaluation scope, and access conditions may change without notice.

---

## Final Notice

Organizations interested in evaluating VRP should make contact while the enterprise pilot window remains open.

Engineering resources are finite.

The priority is building, validating, and improving the protocol—not maintaining an indefinitely open enterprise intake process.

Development will continue regardless of pilot participation.

The repositories remain public.

The evidence remains public.

The protocol continues to evolve.
