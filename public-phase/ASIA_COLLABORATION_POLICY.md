# Asia Collaboration Policy

VRP is an international protocol.

It is not permanently tied to any single country or market.

The next phase of the project may prioritize collaboration with engineering teams in Asia.

This is not a political decision.

It is an engineering decision.

---

## What I Am Looking For

The next phase requires partners who are ready to build.

Not simply observe.

Not endlessly evaluate.

Not wait for someone else to move first.

The priority is working with organizations that are prepared to invest engineering time into real validation, structured feedback, and serious technical collaboration.

---

## Why Asia Is Being Considered

Many organizations across Asia have demonstrated a willingness to move quickly from evaluation to execution.

Speed matters.

Commitment matters.

Engineering capability matters.

The ability to make technical decisions without unnecessary delay matters.

These qualities are more important than geography.

If those conditions exist elsewhere, collaboration remains possible there as well.

---

## Collaboration Principles

Future collaboration will prioritize partners who:

- respect intellectual property
- value original engineering work
- engage through direct technical discussion
- evaluate technology in controlled environments
- provide meaningful engineering feedback
- understand protected-core boundaries

The objective is long-term collaboration, not short-term curiosity.

---

## Current Status

Public pilot intake: closed.

Private evaluation: paused.

Research mode: active.

The protocol continues to evolve.

Future collaboration will be based on engineering quality, trust, and shared commitment—not on public visibility.
